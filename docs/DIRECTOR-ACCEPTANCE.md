# Story editor acceptance — 2026-09-07

Real DeepSeek checks exposed problems that schema-only tests did not: a proposed identity replacement invented a prior radio call and moved Elias from his workshop to the lighthouse; rejected proposals could return incomplete fields; a new character could be missing from the principal cast and include unusable reference URLs.

The editor now receives the exact text-only schema. Model-supplied image/voice URLs are discarded. Candidate IDs are checked against established identities before replacement with application IDs, and declared newcomers receive explicit cast anchors. No candidate becomes published canon at this stage.

Every non-rejected live plan now receives one separately budgeted continuity audit before it is saved for admission. The audit can add owner-review flags and review requirements, but cannot remove a rejection, rewrite a scene or waive an owner decision. A contradiction makes the plan rejected and prevents queue admission. Ambiguity returns for review. Existing publication review of the actual video remains required: two models are not proof of visual consistency or reliable classification in every language.

Both editor calls use the existing database budget guards and retain a conservative 25-cent allowance per call. The generation Workflow preparation step permits two minutes while keeping automatic retries disabled. A malformed response leaves the contribution saved and its call allowance retained. Response assembly copies bounded stream chunks so a reused backing buffer cannot corrupt earlier bytes; a regression test reproduces this buffer-reuse case. Two live response-reading failures were retained during evaluation; the specific cause of those two network responses is not independently proven by the unit reproduction.

All stories and prompts here are isolated fictional fixtures. No fixture was uploaded to the public database, no video was requested or published, and no Paddle operation occurred. DeepSeek authorization is separate from the fal allowance.
