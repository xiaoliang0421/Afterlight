# Policy completion before public launch

The current text in `shared/policies.json` is an authored development draft reflecting the implemented data flow. It is not a claim of legal compliance or a completed public contract.

Before finalizing it, supply the operator’s legal identity, working contact details and intended launch markets. Confirm public age eligibility and safeguards, retention periods with implemented deletion/anonymization, responses to privacy and content complaints, cloud/model processing terms, international transfers, provider input/output retention and any training use. Review the contribution license, attribution changes, shared-story deletion, consumer rights and jurisdiction-specific provisions against the actual operation. Do not promise a deletion deadline, exclusive AI copyright or zero model retention that the system/provider terms cannot support.

`policy_documents` stores the exact text accepted for each version. Every material text or operator-information change must create a new version, with a dated effective policy once approved. Acceptance of a draft does not count as acceptance of a later final version. Users may refuse and continue watching; account/privacy requests and withdrawal of eligible existing tasks stay available. No marketing opt-in or advertising tracking is currently present.

Customer checkout remains disabled. Final purchase, refund, tax and payment-provider terms are a separate gate before charging users; the current free-credit notice is not a paid subscription agreement.
